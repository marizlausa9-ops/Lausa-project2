About KCC

Rooted in faith. Committed to learning. Inspired to serve.

Our Story

A Century of Catholic Education

Established in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has a long-standing role in providing Catholic education in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Through the years, the institution has continued to nurture learners in knowledge, faith, and service.

Inspired by the teaching “Teach Ye All Nations,” KCC seeks to help students become responsible individuals who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

What We Commit To

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Our Identity

Who We Are

KCC is a Catholic learning community where faith, academic growth, and service work together. We encourage students to pursue excellence while living with compassion, integrity, and responsibility.
